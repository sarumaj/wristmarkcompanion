# Device test, 2026-11-17: a laptop's webcam

The first run of the guided test ([protocol](../device-tests.md)), on a Mac laptop's webcam in
Edge 157, with the watch app showing marker 0 under an indoor lamp. Steps 1 to 3 were measured;
the models (step 4) were not.

## What they showed, and what was done

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Done                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **120 FPS** on a 30-frame camera: the viewer detected at every display refresh (120 Hz here), so each camera frame was analyzed about four times. The FPS and the detection rates counted the repeats                                                                        | Detection once per camera frame (`requestVideoFrameCallback`); the drawing goes on at the display's rate. FPS is now the camera frames analyzed a second. A test checks it                     |
| **The distance read short far away** (0.84 of the true distance at 75 to 100 cm) and long close up. Not a wrong field of view, which would scale every distance alike, but too few pixels: detection worked on a 640-pixel copy, where the marker is 13 pixels wide at 75 cm | The detection's width grows toward the camera's own (960, 1280, 1920) while a frame takes under 6 ms, and shrinks above 18 ms. On this laptop (3 ms at 640 pixels) it should reach 960 or 1280 |
| **Lost beyond 75 cm**, from the same 13 pixels                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The same: at 1280 pixels the marker keeps twice the cells' width. To be measured again                                                                                                         |
| **Dims after −4 s:** "It dimmed now" was pressed before the marker was first seen: the display was already dim at the start                                                                                                                                                  | Such a press counts as 0 s, with the note "dim before the marker was seen"                                                                                                                     |
| **The display went off after 1 to 2 s** when started by opening the app or a press, after 26 s with the auto-cycle on                                                                                                                                                        | Open: the watch's display mode (always on, or on a gesture) and its timeout weren't noted. The next test notes them; the auto-cycle seems to keep the display on longer                        |
| **Lost at 30 cm in red light** (2 %) while 15 and 75 cm worked, and **48 % head-on** with the normal display                                                                                                                                                                 | Open: likely the lamp's glare on the watch's glass, or the camera's exposure on the bright display. The next test notes the glare; the debug view shows the detector's view                    |
