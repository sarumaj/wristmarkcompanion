# The models

50 models, one for each of the watch's markers. The watch's down and up buttons (or a swipe)
step through them; the viewer names the one it sees at the bottom. Many respond to your wrist,
a tap or your other hand ([user guide](guide.md#move-your-wrist)).

- **The sky is today's:** the moon's phase, the Earth's day and night, the planets and the
  seasons follow the date and time; nothing asks for your location. `?date=2026-12-24T18:00Z` on
  the viewer's address shows another moment.
- **The attitude indicator, the maze and the penguin** follow how the watch is held. On a phone,
  **Use the phone's tilt** under them takes the real horizon from its motion sensors.
