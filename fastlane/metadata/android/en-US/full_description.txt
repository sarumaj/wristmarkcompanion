Your watch, in 3D. WristMarkCompanion shows a marker on your smartwatch's display; point your phone's camera at it, and a 3D model stands on your wrist and follows it as you move.

50 models, one per marker: the moon in tonight's phase, the Earth with day and night as they are now, the planets as they stand today, a world clock, a watch movement showing the time, an hourglass, an attitude indicator that follows your wrist, a helicopter, a rocket launch, a Ferris wheel lit at night, a bonsai in today's season, dice, a marble maze you tilt, fireworks, a snow globe, a campfire, an aquarium, a lighthouse, a puzzle cube that solves itself, a black hole, a robot, a penguin and more.

Move your wrist and they react: flick it to roll the dice or launch a firework, shake it to stir the snow or the fire, twist it to move the planets a month. Tap a model, or pinch to resize it.

Pair the app with the watch through the camera, and it steers the watch: the next model, the automatic cycle, a buzz to find it. The messages are end-to-end encrypted.

What you need: the WristMarkCompanion watch app on your smartwatch. No watch at hand? The app's "Markers without a watch" page shows the markers on any other screen.

Privacy: the camera image is processed on your phone and never leaves it. No account, no ads, no tracking. Details: https://wristmark.apps.ciepiela.org/privacy.html
